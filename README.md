# Riyalvest

**Riyalvest** is an educational app designed for anyone who wants to learn how to invest in the stock market — without taking any financial risks.  

With **Riyalvest**, you get a *virtual capital* to trade **real stocks** using live market data through powerful APIs. Build your portfolio, track your holdings, and explore detailed analytics for every stock you trade.  

## Key Features
- 📊 **Trade Real Stocks with Virtual Money** – Learn the market without risking your own funds.
- 💼 **Personal Portfolio** – Keep track of all your trades in one place.
- 📈 **Stock Analysis** – Access in-depth analytics and insights for each stock.
- ⚡ **Real-Time Data** – Powered by APIs for an authentic trading experience.
